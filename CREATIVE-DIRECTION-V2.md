# REAL MALL OF AMERICA — CREATIVE DIRECTION V2

## Status
**The original generic SVG graphics are rejected and must not be used in production.** This document supersedes the initial visual direction. Do not deploy generated mockup screenshots as storefront UI.

## Brand objective
Build a credible, sophisticated, multi-department ecommerce destination. Design should be editorial, visually cohesive, conversion-focused, accessible, fast, and trustworthy. Avoid imitation of physical Mall of America branding or implying an affiliation.

## Art direction
- Photography: full-bleed, high-resolution lifestyle and architectural campaigns, tactile product details, deliberate lighting, restrained post-production, and authentic human contexts.
- Visual system: ink-black and deep midnight surfaces, warm neutral canvas, muted metallic accent used sparingly. No fake gold gradients, clip-art, repetitive icon cards, or gratuitous shadows.
- Typography: distinctive licensed editorial display face with highly readable sans-serif UI. Use real font licenses and locally hosted/subset WOFF2 assets.
- Motion: 5–8-second seamless hero film, quiet parallax and reveal transitions, static poster fallback, reduced-motion support, and bandwidth-conscious video delivery.
- Mobile-first: optimized art direction, intentional image crops, legible overlays, no text baked into campaign images.

## Production asset manifest
1. Identity: primary and compact logos, monochrome versions, favicon, social avatar, email marks, brand book.
2. Home: 2 desktop hero stills, 2 mobile hero stills, 1 looping hero film + poster, 4 editorial promotional campaigns.
3. Nine departments: desktop and mobile category photography, square thumbnails, social crops, promotional details for each.
4. Commerce UI: product-card treatment, search empty states, account/cart imagery, editorial navigation, subtle motion.
5. Seasonal campaigns: 4 seasonal hero concepts with corresponding email/social variants.
6. Trust: restrained original shipping/returns/service iconography; do not claim free shipping, verified suppliers, or guarantees unless verified.
7. Performance: AVIF/WebP srcsets, posters, lazy loading, accessibility alt text, contrast testing.

## Shopify delivery gates
- Audit actual collection handles, suppliers, theme source, policies, and store name.
- Back up existing theme and work on unpublished duplicate.
- Preserve all real products, collections, pricing, cart and checkout.
- Publish only after responsive QA, accessibility, performance, and purchase-flow verification.

## Asset policy
Generated images are creative concepts until reviewed for product accuracy, brand/IP conflicts, legibility, and conversion into production assets. No fake brand logos or unsupported claims.
