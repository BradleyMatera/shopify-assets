# Editorial retail system

Photo-led merchandising; midnight ink, warm ivory and restrained bronze; generous spacing; clear search and department navigation; rectangular controls rather than pill-shaped buttons.

## Actual deliverables
See `assets/` for PNG identities and interface icons, JPEG/WebP photographs, backgrounds, campaign banners, social/email artwork, and encoded MP4/WebM motion. `production/asset-manifest.json` records only files actually created. Source photography and generation provenance are in `assets/masters/` and `production/sources.json`.

## Image rules
Use the largest appropriate native-detail export. Do not claim 4K detail from smaller originals. Responsive crops are explicit derivatives, not additional original shoots. Generated product-like objects in campaign scenes do not establish a SKU, supplier, warranty or stock availability. The architectural scene is fictional editorial art, not evidence of premises ownership or a relationship with the physical Mall of America.

## Identity
PNG wordmarks are provided in horizontal and stacked arrangements, along with monochrome/bronze marks, favicons and social avatars. Raster lettering is rendered from available fonts; no font files are distributed. Use the existing theme's properly licensed typography settings for live text.

## Merchandising integrity
No fabricated ratings, discounts, shipping promises, security seals, supplier approvals or brand affiliations. Product media and policies remain authoritative.
