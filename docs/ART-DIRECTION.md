# Premium Retail Art Direction

## Objective
A modern national-scale digital marketplace, visually credible and commercially useful. Editorial restraint instead of imitation luxury: strong composition, real merchandise, elegant hierarchy, tactile materials, natural human settings, and decisive whitespace.

## Photography
- Use licensed, commissioned, or clearly original imagery.
- Shoot or generate campaigns with specific narrative and compositional briefs; no random stock collage.
- Editorial daylight, authentic materials, dimensional lighting, natural skin and fabric detail.
- No baked-in headline text. Product images must depict actual products, not lookalikes.

## Typography
A distinctive properly licensed display family plus highly legible UI sans. Define optical sizes, weights, tracking, and responsive type scale. Do not use decorative gold fonts or fake brand seals.

## Interface
Fast product discovery, clear department navigation, accurate product tiles, sophisticated filters, useful search, transparent delivery/returns information, and accessible checkout.

## Motion
Short seamless muted film loops with poster fallbacks. No autoplay with sound. Honor reduced motion and bandwidth constraints.

## Visual quality bar
The site must look like one coherent art-directed brand, not a collage of independently generated images. Require manual editorial review before release.
