# Matera Digital Boutique | Organic creative sprint
Ten zero-spend, 7–10-second motion concepts for the existing Boutique artwork. These are scripts and directions, not rendered films or scheduled posts. Use your own footage if available; otherwise animate the supplied illustrative editorial crops honestly. Do not imply that a scene shows a product for sale. Use the approved pine #142B22, ivory #F7F3EA, clay #9C4E38 and apricot #E4AD89 palette, existing wordmark, Georgia editorial headlines and Arial supporting text.

**Build once, adapt honestly:** Start with the numbered files in `[local working file]`. Cut at 1080×1920 for vertical video with safe margins; the existing 4:5, square, 2:3 and landscape stills are channel alternatives, not automatic crops of the video. Hold the opening line long enough to read, use dialogue captions if voiced, work in silence, and avoid flashed transitions. A 2-second end card holds the wordmark and one CTA. Use the verified Boutique homepage or a freshly checked matching guide; no exaggerated promise, pricing, discounts, delivery, stock, affiliate claim, or music licensing assumptions. Add a short illustrative-image disclosure when the scene could look like a listing.
