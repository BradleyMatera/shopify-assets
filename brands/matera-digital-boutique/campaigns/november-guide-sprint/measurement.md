# Matera Digital Boutique: organic sprint measurement

## Aim and scope

Prove people can discover the Boutique, reach the intended page, and purchase a verified product. Run ten genuinely different concepts, each adapted to each verified business channel. Do not turn ten concepts into ten copies of one ad or count three adaptations as three concepts. Launch at most one post per channel per day at first; review each before expanding distribution. No paid spend.

## Give every concept a distinct job

| ID | Concept | Primary action | Best leading signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Boutique introduction | See the storefront | Unique outbound clicks |
| C02 | Kitchen routine checklist | Keep or open the guide | Saves per reach |
| C03 | Before/after counter organization | Open the guide for steps | Outbound clicks per eligible impressions |
| C04 | How to compare towel pack sizes | Compare real options | Product views from eligible referrals |
| C05 | A low-clutter kitchen question | Share a specific routine | Relevant comments per reach |
| C06 | The care-instructions explainer | Open the guide | Saves per reach |
| C07 | A real shopping FAQ | Read the matching policy/guide | Unique outbound clicks |
| C08 | A two-option gift decision | Open the curated guide | Outbound clicks per eligible impressions |
| C09 | The sizing and fit checklist | Save and consult later | Saves per reach |
| C10 | The verified product feature | View the exact sellable variant | Verified add-to-cart and checkout starts |

Use only verified facts. A guide CTA can run without a cleared SKU; product-led C04 and C10 wait until the exact variant, pricing, stock, shipping and checkout work. No invented reviews, certifications, deadlines, guarantees or discounts.

## Platform distribution and tracking

- **Facebook:** Localized copy for the existing business Page, 4:5 image, one direct tagged link; use native views/reach, meaningful comments and outbound clicks. No personal profile or connected personal Instagram.
- **Instagram:** An independent professional Boutique account, short captions or carousel, prominent bio or Stories link when available. Feed engagement and bio/story referral clicks are different measures. If several posts share one static bio URL, attribute the referral to the account/placement rather than a specific post.
- **Pinterest:** Independent business account, vertical creative and search-led title/description, one destination per Pin. Log saves and outbound clicks separately from closeups. Keep personal profiles/catalogs intact and do not migrate a catalog on stale diagnostics.
- **Other channels:** Add only after business account and public click path are independently verified; do not count planning as availability or use someone’s personal account.

Example: `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mdb_organic_launch&utm_content=c01_intro_feed`. Keep the same campaign ID across adaptations, but keep source and content/placement values distinct. Use the new primary host and retain the exact variant query in product URLs. Confirm the destination and tags survive redirects before release. Test links without checking out; exclude operator QA from sales attribution wherever distinguishable. Retain analytics access boundaries and no personal customer details in shared reports.

## Evidence and gates

1. **Ready:** Business profile identity, matching artwork, landing path, offer checks (if applicable) and published policy links independently verified; placement-specific caption, alt text, tags and target slot recorded.
2. **Scheduled:** Native platform confirms the future status and slot. Record it separately from posts live.
3. **Published:** Native publication and a public permalink both verified. Record platform, concept ID, publish time (Central), permalink, tested destination, and spend of $0. Count up to ten uniquely published concepts per eligible channel; failed, deleted, drafts and queued posts do not count.
4. **Attributed traffic:** Record native reporting window and metric definitions; use Shopify or configured analytics for eligible campaign sessions and funnel events. Report missing tagging/permission as unknown. Never sum platform reach into unique people or use engagement as a revenue proxy.
5. **Paid order:** A completed, non-test Shopify order with matching tagged session or another documented source. Report source-linked orders separately from unattributed orders. Track cancellations/refunds separately. Don't treat cart events, pending payments, payout balances or sales claims as paid orders. Record gross product sales, discounts/returns and net sales separately from tax/shipping and report margins only with actual cost/fees and settlement evidence.

## Review thresholds (operating rules, not claimed results)

- **Stop immediately** for broken URL, wrong personal/business identity, wrong variants or prices, unverifiable factual claims, out-of-stock featured variant, policy contradiction, unintentional spend, or a checkout blocker. Correct and independently retest the exact path before resuming.
- **Review after 48–72 hours** per post for reach, engagement and link integrity; use a seven-day reporting window where supported, with lag explicitly labeled. A missing or delayed metric is not zero.
- **If under 100 eligible impressions or 20 attributable landing sessions per candidate,** treat rates as directional only. Do not call winners/losers, publish more of the same blindly, or conclude the product cannot sell. Compare within a platform and placement with the same window and definitions.
- **If at least 100 eligible impressions and no outbound click,** inspect CTA, destination and content/placement match. Change one variable or shift that concept to a better channel instead of boosting.
- **If at least 20 attributable landing sessions and no product view/add-to-cart on a product-intent destination,** validate event instrumentation and mobile first fold; then test landing/product match. Useful traffic with no basket signal is not a purchase-path diagnosis by itself.
- **Any first verified positive net outcome** merits review, not a blanket scale decision. Expand only when the content is materially distinct, publishing path is reliable, and stock/price/freight/margin gates still pass.

First report: one row per channel for roles running vs completed, created, scheduled, verified live, spend, reported impressions, attributable sessions and paid orders; then one row per concept/channel with links and observations. Show real counts and denominator/observation time. No historical baselines until the source windows and bot/QA exclusion are verified.
