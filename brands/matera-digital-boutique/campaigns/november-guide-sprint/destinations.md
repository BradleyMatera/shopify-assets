## Use and verify before pushing creative
- Keep post copy editorial (read/compare/measure) and avoid quoting prices, promoting product or collection stock, certifications, coupon codes, and delivery claims.
- Native product shelves are not part of these landing choices; each article body includes an optional generic **Home & Kitchen** collection link. It is not the ad destination and is not a qualified Boutique-only assortment. Lead approved traffic to exact articles, not to the broad collection. The visual kitchen-counter feature article was excluded: it still includes two specific product links outside the launch scope.
- The ten canonical URLs lack explicit `og:image` and `twitter:image`; attach the verified premade art in the post instead of relying on autogenerated link cards. Do not set up a new catalog or tag products to compensate.
- Canonicals lack query strings as expected. Preserve `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=boutique_guide_sprint`, and the row’s distinct `utm_content` plus the real per-platform `utm_source` (facebook / instagram / pinterest). Use the official profile or Story link for Instagram; feed caption URL is not a substitute for a clickable destination.
- Shopify storefront analytics framework was visible. HTML/HTTP cannot verify consent, event receipt, deduplication, or attribution. Before counting traffic, use a single consent-appropriate human QA click with a distinct `utm_content=qa_do_not_count`. Check matching human session/landing data in native reporting; avoid any fake order or simulated purchase. Count engagement, tagged sessions and orders separately.
