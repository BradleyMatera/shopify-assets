# Matera Digital Boutique | Black Friday organic creative

Three distinct options. Pick one for immediate manual publication once the authorized business Page is confirmed. No scheduling, boost, discount, stock, timer, or shipping promise. Use the same graphic only once. The asset filenames point to existing Boutique artwork, not sale badges or product photos.

## 1. Buy the right thing, not the quickest thing

**Shopper point:** A deal is not useful if the item doesn’t fit. Take the three-measurement check into any Black Friday shopping.
**Graphic headline:** MEASURE THE SPACE, NOT THE DEAL.
**Facebook copy:** A Black Friday deal isn’t much use if it won’t fit. Before buying a kitchen organizer, write down the inside width, depth, and height. If it goes in a cabinet, measure the opening at the hinge too. [Browse the buying guides.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/pages/buying-guide-library?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=black_friday_editorial&utm_content=fit_first)
**Existing art:** `organic-social-30/facebook-03-measure-first.png` (Counter Edit, “Measure First”); the exact branded source is `brands/matera-digital-boutique/production-kit/social/exports/counter-edit--facebook-feed--brand.png` in the graphics repository.
**Short film, 12 seconds:** 0–2s opening card; 2–9s tape measure across width, depth and height using an unbranded drawer; 9–12s show handwritten dimensions and “Buying guides at Matera Digital Boutique.” Captions on, no shopping-cart countdown.

## 2. The listing photo is not the packing list

**Shopper point:** Help customers avoid ordering the wrong option or mistaking photo props for included items.
**Graphic headline:** WHAT’S ACTUALLY IN THE BOX?
**Facebook copy:** Before you check out on Black Friday, give the listing one last look: Which option did you select, how many pieces does it include, and what will fit in your space? Trust the written details over the styling in the photo. [Open the buying guides.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/pages/buying-guide-library?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=black_friday_editorial&utm_content=check_the_pack)
**Existing art:** `organic-social-30/facebook-05-pack-check.png` (Counter Edit, “Check the Pack”); branded source `brands/matera-digital-boutique/production-kit/social/exports/counter-edit--facebook-feed--brand.png`.
**Short film, 14 seconds:** 0–3s overlay “Photo ≠ packing list”; 3–10s tick OPTION / COUNT / SIZE on a paper list; 10–14s show “Check the written details” and the Boutique guide destination. No fake listing, retailer screenshot or merchandise claim.

## 3. One useful gift beats a bigger cart

**Shopper point:** Give Black Friday browsing a specific job: solve a real, recurring problem for the gift recipient.
**Graphic headline:** SHOP THEIR ROUTINE, NOT THE SALE.
**Facebook copy:** Here’s a Black Friday gift rule: think of what they actually do every week. Cook for a crowd? Always have friends over? Start with one small thing that would make that routine easier. [Browse Matera Digital Boutique.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=black_friday_editorial&utm_content=one_useful_gift)
**Existing art:** `organic-social-30/facebook-10-simple-gift.png` (Market Notes, “A Useful Gift”); branded source `brands/matera-digital-boutique/production-kit/social/exports/market-notes--facebook-feed--brand.png`.
**Short film, 15 seconds:** 0–3s ask “What do they do every week?”; 3–11s jot “cook / host / clean up” on three handwritten cards and circle one; 11–15s end “One useful thing > more stuff” over an editorial still. Don’t present mood props as merchandise.

**Pick:** Option 1 is the strongest first post: one immediately useful action, an existing on-message graphic, and a guide CTA rather than a made-up deal.
