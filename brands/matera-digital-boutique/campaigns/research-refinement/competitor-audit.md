# Home and Kitchen Competitive Creative Audit
**Research date:** November 27, 2026. **Purpose:** identify original, low-cost creative formats that help shoppers choose. This is design research, not a demand estimate or a sales forecast.

## Test recommendations: evidence to collect on our own account
1. **Lead test:** compare a buyer-choice opener (show two actual pack sizes, exact count) with a useful-task opener (show a real kitchen reset). Keep image crop, landing destination, publishing surface and measurement windows comparable; make the opener the primary deliberate change. If tying either to a SKU, recheck the actual selected variant, checkout availability and terms immediately before release.
2. **Format:** film original hands/counter footage, show what the viewer gets in the opening seconds, caption for sound-off viewing, then use one CTA to a relevant current page. Use instructional carousel and single-image checklist variants with unique purpose, not duplicated posts. No competitor imagery, text, logos, audio, implied endorsements or unverifiable discount/stock/certification claims.
3. **Measurement:** use distinct tagged URLs and creative IDs. Report reach/views where available, outbound clicks, verified storefront sessions, add-to-carts, checkout starts and paid orders separately. Flag staff tests; report numerator and denominator for rates. Analyze 24-hour and seven-day snapshots for equal age. Low counts are directional only: no winner, revenue projection, or claim of causation from isolated clicks.
4. **Scope:** release organically through verified business accounts only. No fabricated comments, copied ads, paid boosts, personal accounts or engagements made solely to inflate metrics.
