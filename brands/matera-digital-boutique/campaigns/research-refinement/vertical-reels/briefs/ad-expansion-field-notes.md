# 50 Boutique creative briefs: demonstrations, not caption variants

These are **50 pre-production briefs**, not filmed or scheduled ads. Each has a different shopper decision and a visible test or reveal. Format describes the intended original composition, not three exports of one asset. The destination ledger below uses existing guide pages; recheck exact URL and supporting subsection before any release. Use unbranded props you own. No supplier imagery, listing promises, urgency, pricing, comparative claims, or paid promotion. Where props could be mistaken for products for sale, label the scene “Illustrative setup.” Add platform-native clickable link placement and human-readable alt text to final assets. Reel subtitles must make the story work on mute.

## Production discipline

Maintain an ID-based shot and receipt ledger. Preflight no-identifying-information in scene, original or approved media, correct brand identity and link, at least one genuine useful demonstration, no listed offer claim, publisher identity, mobile legibility and measurement plan. For a real first batch, prioritize M02, M13, S11, P08 and P14 after the additional guide checks where required: different jobs and camera grammar, not five versions of the same post. Keep alternate crops and copied captions out of the unique ad count. No submission, schedule, or publication is implied.
