# Matera Digital Boutique: 25 hands-on commercial creative briefs

All assets are **briefs**, not filmed clips, drafted posts, scheduled items, or paid ads. Use original footage, owned unbranded props, and the approved Boutique identity. These concepts cover new shopper jobs beyond the original ten pantry/kitchen/bath/entry/closet/workstation campaigns; the adjacent categories are editorial experiments, not additions to the curated shopping assortment.

Every exact destination below was independently fetched over HTTPS and returned 200, was present in the live Buying Guide Library, self-canonicalized on the Boutique hostname, showed a substantive headline/body, and contained no direct product link or old mall name in its main content. This confirms live destinations only. A guide may include a broad optional collection link; do not present that link as a vetted shopping list. Two otherwise usable guides were excluded because they include unqualified direct product links. Never use a product price, delivery claim, sizing guarantee, medical claim or discount unless separately approved for that exact live item.

Produce vertical handheld **9:16** footage or storyboard frames with the useful action in the opening shot, fixed/consistent comparison settings, restrained voiceover or captions, readable text on mobile, and clean original sound or silence. The on-camera proof below is a production requirement, not evidence already recorded. Use a scoped tagged URL for each platform and a distinct lower-case row ID as `utm_content`; use the existing campaign taxonomy rather than renaming in production. Publish only to authorized business destinations; no feed autopublish or spend.


### c01. The handle counts
- **Buyer job:** Avoid choosing cabin luggage using an outdated dimensional rule.
- **Shot/action:** Single take: measure a plain packed bag from its widest points, including wheels and handles; cut to a blank itinerary-specific allowance checklist.
- **Proof required:** Show all three real measurements and clearly label the checklist a prompt, not an airline rule; check the current airline rules independently if named.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Read the carry-on measuring guide.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/carry-on-luggage-measure-against-your-airline-s-rules)

### c02. Two bags, one trunk
- **Buyer job:** Choose a bag structure for the way you load and reach it rather than assuming either construction is always better.
- **Shot/action:** Overhead split-screen: make dimension-matched cardboard silhouettes; use paper hinges for a center-opening setup versus a top flap in the same taped footprint.
- **Proof required:** Same simulated footprint and labeled opening styles; no impact, waterproofing, capacity, or durability comparison.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Compare luggage layouts for your trip.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/hard-shell-or-soft-sided-luggage-compare-the-trip)

### c03. Unpack one change of clothes
- **Buyer job:** Choose packing organization that keeps a needed item retrievable without emptying the whole bag.
- **Shot/action:** POV retrieval test: lift a single existing pouch from a tray of folded clothes, then replace it as packed; overlay “what gets unpacked?”
- **Proof required:** Same number of garments in each comparison, one continuous before/after retrieval, no space-savings or speed promise.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Read the packing-cube and pouch guide.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/packing-cubes-and-travel-pouches-choose-a-system-not-more-bulk)

### c04. One plug is not a plan
- **Buyer job:** Choose a travel charging kit for the actual devices, connections, and outlet arrangement.
- **Shot/action:** Macro tabletop: arrange phone, laptop, camera silhouettes beside unplugged connector samples; put a check by each verified manual entry.
- **Proof required:** Show labeled mechanical differences and generic prompts; no energized demo, unsupported voltage adapter, fast-charge, or airline claim.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Build your charger and cable checklist.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/travel-chargers-and-cables-build-a-compatibility-list)

### c05. The doorway test
- **Buyer job:** Check whether a daily bag suits the commute, items, and access habits rather than relying on liters alone.
- **Shot/action:** Narrative close-up: put your ordinary daily items in an unbranded bag, close it, then try reaching your keys from outside a doorway.
- **Proof required:** Film with the same contents for each setup; focus on user behavior, not comfort, safety, or manufacturer specifications.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Read the everyday bag checklist.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/everyday-bags-check-capacity-closure-and-carry-comfort)

### c06. Where the tape sits
- **Buyer job:** Prevent a guess based on an “one size” designation or another maker’s chart.
- **Shot/action:** Mirror shot: run a soft tape where a hat would rest, then draw three neutral crown profiles on paper.
- **Proof required:** Show actual placement and compare to the intended manufacturer chart privately; don’t include size guarantees or use an invented official chart.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Read the hat fit guide.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/find-your-hat-size-fit-closure-and-crown-shape)

### c07. Three outfits, two pieces
- **Buyer job:** Build a small, frequently worn assortment around actual outfit choices.
- **Shot/action:** Wardrobe flat lay: cycle three owned outfit silhouettes while two unbranded jewelry props move into the frames that use them.
- **Proof required:** Show repeat combinations and distinguish personal styling from supposed universal rules. No metals, price, or quality claim.

### c08. Read the whole material line
- **Buyer job:** Tell finish from base material before buying online.

- **Proof required:** Labels marked examples; no genuine-metal test, allergen claim, colorfastness, or recommendation for sensitive skin.

### c09. Read the cuff and the chart
- **Buyer job:** Choose socks for footwear and preferred fit, not just a generic size band.
- **Shot/action:** Macro: turn plain socks inside out and show seam, height, and thickness; compare a pair beside the intended existing shoes.
- **Proof required:** Show what is actually visible; no blister, circulation, sweat, durability, or compression claims.
- **CTA & exact public guide:** [Compare sock fit and construction.](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/blogs/news/sock-shopping-compare-size-fabric-and-construction)
