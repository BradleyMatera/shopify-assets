## Execution and honest measurement
- First test Pinterest's direct-link organic Pins and Facebook Page link posts only when the correct business identities and click paths are confirmed. Other channels are discovery-only until an outbound surface is verified; don't inflate drafts into scheduled or live posts.
- Test two distinct, truthful concepts for the **same** landing product or guide: “Towel drawer reset” (utility) vs. “A counter reset in three steps” (narrative). Native crop/captions and platform-specific copy. Change one major variable at a time; don't post repetitive content, farm accounts or flood hashtags.
- Use the Boutique primary host, exact product variant when applicable, and organic attribution: utm_source=platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mdb_counter_edit, utm_content=unique_creative_key. Verify native permalink and actual link in the platform preview. Use [local build path omitted] as a lint/landing check; a manifest row is not independent platform proof.
- Separate reach → actual outbound clicks/taps → tagged human sessions → carts → checkouts → orders. Report denominators and distinguish QA/bots. Compare similar elapsed windows (e.g. 48 hours creative, seven days downstream); this is an evaluation proposal, not a ranking rule. Tiny traffic without orders cannot prove checkout failure. No paid spend, new subscriptions, unsupported claims or fake engagement.

## Creative acceptance rubric
Hold a creative if any step fails.
1. **Identity and truth:** correct Boutique label, audience problem, one product/guide and one CTA. Supporting evidence for variant, stock, price, shipping or returns statements. No unproven certification, environmental, scarcity or discount promise. Verify visual/music rights; generated editorial scenes cannot stand in for actual product proof.
2. **Mobile creative and accessibility:** useful first frame, clear text, captions, readable contrast and in-app safe areas. No reused watermarks, fake reviews, glitchy visual claims or private data. Inspect a real compressed preview.
3. **Conversion path:** tap actual link, verify correct new host, exact live path/variant, visible price and ordering path. Verify unique organic UTM values. A product page load is not a placed order or proof of paid checkout.
4. **Publication integrity:** right native account and disclosure, correct timezone, draft only if assembled, scheduled only with platform evidence, live only with native post/permalink and observed status. Record failures and unpublishes separately.
5. **Decision quality:** record asset/hook, legal or fulfillment constraints, and real results. Scale only when credible downstream intent and margin/stock conditions warrant it.
