# Matera Digital Boutique: Free acquisition beyond product feeds

## What is live and what is not

- The [Boutique storefront](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/) and [Buying Guide Library](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/pages/buying-guide-library) returned public HTTP 200. The ten editorial destinations in the acquisition sprint each returned 200, self-canonicalized on the new domain, and preserved sampled UTM query strings. They are editorial destinations, not offers of verified inventory. The broad collection links inside guides are not the campaign destinations.
- Public [robots.txt](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/robots.txt) and [sitemap](https://materadigitalboutique.myshopify.com/sitemap.xml) returned 200 and the sitemap indexes six child files on the new domain, including articles. Old home and an old pantry-guide URL each reached the matching new path in a single redirect. This is sampled technical evidence, not proof of indexation or of every historical path.
- The storefront and guide hub render a first-party Shopify newsletter input. This proves a form is present, not a verified subscription, usable list, or verified sending identity.
- The [owned public portfolio](https://bradleymatera.dev/) still has a live “Explore the shop” slot linked to the old domain and described as tech/workspace/everyday finds. Its current redirect works; the link and wording have not been updated by this audit.
- Google organic search (Search Console) and product eligibility (Merchant Center) are separate workstreams. Google Shopping/free product listings are not ready while verification and approved catalog scope remain open. Do not hold organic guide discovery waiting for a Merchant Center approval.

## Report real results, not activity

Keep one campaign taxonomy already used on the verified guides. For SEO, use Search Console impressions, clicks, query/page and index coverage rather than forcing UTMs into canonical URLs. For named owned links and consented email, use source / medium / campaign / creative or slot. Filter Shopify reports to human-classified sessions where the dimension exists and exclude marked QA traffic; measure tagged sessions and real paid orders separately. The presence of tracking code, link previews, editorial exports, or submissions is not a visit, customer, or sale. See [Shopify marketing reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/marketing-reports) and [Shopify bot filtering](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/bots/bot-filtering).

No outreach, publication, account change, scheduling, spend, or subscription test was performed.
