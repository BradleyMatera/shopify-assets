## Before each placement

1. Confirm the dedicated account name, avatar and destination. If copy or media only exists in this repository, it is not live.
2. Preview on mobile, including feed/board thumbnails, native controls and final captions. Recheck the exact storefront link in a signed-out tab. Put links in the native website, Story sticker or Pin destination field, not in the image.
3. Keep evergreen guidance accurate. No discounts, stock, delivery guarantees, personal details, or filmed-demo claims. Publish organically only, with no budget, boost, schedule or cross-post to the personal profile.
4. After an authorized publish, save the public permalink and verify the content and destination signed out before recording the placement as live.
