## Internal release checks

- Verify the correct merchant, order, item, exact SKU and payment state before answering.
- Check the current storefront policy for the order date and the order’s actual fulfillment. Supplier retail terms are not customer terms. Never promise fees, a label, an exchange, a refund amount, a warranty, or an arrival date before verification.
- Never ask for card details, passwords, or a code. Do not ask for more personal data than necessary.
- Reply through the verified support channel. Update the actual ticket or order record. Test inbox routing before enabling any transactional notification changes.
