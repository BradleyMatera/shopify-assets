# Voice and art direction
**Position:** a focused shop for the things you reach for in the kitchen and at the market. The buying information should be as thoughtful as the look.
**Voice:** warm, direct, understated. Show one useful job at a time. Prefer exact pack sizes and dimensions to “premium,” “best” or “must have.” Avoid fake discounts, fake countdowns, vague environmental promises, guaranteed arrival and unverified materials.
**Design:** pine #142B22, ivory #F7F3EA, clay #9C4E38, apricot #E4AD89. On small text default to pine on ivory or ivory on pine. Use clay only with tested backgrounds. Use no more than one warm accent at a time. Keep whitespace, an editorial hierarchy and uncluttered crops. Type and mark details live in identity/.
**Storefront headline:** Useful things for every day.
**Campaign distinction:** “Counter Edit” for clear information on kitchen routines; “Market List” for compact shopping and carry use cases. Brand-only scenes are atmosphere. No exact-item claim without real supplier-approved assets.
**Examples:** “Check the pack before you check out.” “Know which size you’re getting.” “Kitchen staples, with the details up front.”
